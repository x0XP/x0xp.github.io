Quick Guide
• Search any item or boss name.
• Enter to see price or boss uniques.
• Right‑click (or long‑press on mobile) a recent search to pin it above.
• Use the share icon to copy a link with a live Discord embed.
• The wiki icon opens the official OSRS Wiki page.
• Drag the top edge of a price card to create a floating copy.
• Click the clipboard icon beside the checker title for updates.
